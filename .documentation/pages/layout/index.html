Your long-standing VTuber rival is headed to the same convention as you...but wait, she's actually cute IRL?!

Become a rising indie VTuber and finally visit the biggest convention in the industry — but a few drinks too deep into a networking party, you realize you've actually befriended your biggest online rival! Will this crash into a scandal, or will true love bloom instead?

My VTuber Rival is Actually Cute?! is a short slice-of-life yuri visual novel love letter to the VTuber & streaming industry.

Demo features:

  • customize your female protagonist, including your VTuber streaming preferences, in-game textbox, and more
  • meet your love interest Anne, who is definitely not secretly your VTuber rival Angelilyca
  • under an hour prelude to a comfy romance, created by industry veterans

Full game features:

  • customize your female protagonist's VTuber persona and IRL design, including your streaming preferences, in-game textbox, and more
  • 1 incredibly cute girl to fall in love with
  • over 30k words of a comfy romance, created by industry veterans
