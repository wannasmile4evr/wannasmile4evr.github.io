## NOTE

WannaSmile now operates under ticketed usage, ticket approval is permanent, once a ticket is created, you now own that ticket, the status of your ticket may change due to your activity in comments, and behavior, the 1st 50 tickets will be auto-approved, those after will have to check if they've been approved, a ticket is required for practically everything inside of WannaSmile but is to avoid bot traffic & you won't even have to ever use it after its creation and approval thereof.
