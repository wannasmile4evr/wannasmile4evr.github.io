WannaSmile4Evr

WannaSmile4Evr is an independent, ad-free collection of playable browser games — arcade throwbacks, visual novels, oddities pulled from every corner of the web, and a few in-house pages besides. No accounts, no installs: pick something and it just runs.

The library and its themes are rebuilt continuously, so the shelf looks a little different every time you check back.
