DMCA Policy

How copyright takedown requests are handled on this site.

← Back to site
WannaSmile4Evr hosts and links to third-party web games. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, you can request its removal under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). Before filing, a valid notice has to meet the three requirements below — this mirrors the notice-and-takedown standard set out in 17 U.S.C. §512, the same rule every service provider (site host, search engine, ISP) operates under.

The 3 rules of filing a DMCA notice

1

Identify the copyrighted work

Name the specific work you own the rights to — the game, its assets, or the content you're claiming was used without permission. Vague claims ("something on this site") can't be acted on.

2

Identify the infringing material and its location

Give the exact page or asset URL on this site where the material appears, so it can actually be found and reviewed.

3

Provide contact info and a good-faith statement

Include your name and a way to reach you, plus a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized and that the information you're providing is accurate — under penalty of perjury.

File a takedown request

Filing opens a new GitHub issue on this site's repository, pre-filled with the DMCA template — fill in the specific asset, the work it infringes, and your contact information there.
